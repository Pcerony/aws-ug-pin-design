# Silver Wafer Exploration

Mode: built-in image_gen. Reference: specs/approved/badge-1-reference.png (outline only).

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: face-on material design exploration, not a factory specification.
Primary request: A fixed 80 x 80 mm pentagonal badge made from diced black silicon wafer dies. Bright selected individual dies TOGETHER FORM THE NUMBER 1. It is absolutely not a solid numeral placed on a tiled background.
Input image 1: reference for exact outline geometry only. Fill its pentagon, discard its black exterior and use a pure white exterior.
Composition: Square image, strictly orthographic face-on. Pentagon spans the entire square canvas with zero border padding, touching canvas at top tip, left/right shoulders and bottom edge. Exact normalized vertices (0.5,0), (1,0.5), (0.925,1), (0.075,1), (0,0.5). Thin plain dark metallic rim, no perspective, no cast shadow.
Structure: Inside the polygon an orthogonal 9-column by 10-row grid of square/rectangular silicon semiconductor dies, clipped cleanly at the angled perimeter. Narrow continuous straight black dicing grooves separate all cells. Black and charcoal dies occupy the majority of the surface, with subtle recessed layered micro-etched geometric semiconductor circuit patterns, not noisy circuitry.
Numeral: Select a contiguous set of complete existing grid cells to form a large readable number 1, a short stair-step flag at the upper left leading to a thick straight vertical stem and a wide horizontal base. Rough cell layout: row 3 columns 4 and 5 bright, row 4 columns 3, 4 and 5 bright, rows 5 through 8 columns 4 and 5 bright, row 9 columns 2 through 7 bright. All other cells dark. Preserve the same narrow black grid seams through EVERY bright cell, visibly breaking the numeral into individual dies. No numeral outline, no overlay, no separate raised solid number.
Materials: Selected numeral dies are reflective platinum and titanium silver with subtle blue-tinted silicon reflections; elegant transitions from bright brushed silver to gunmetal across individual cells, a few tiny champagne contact pads WITHIN silver cells. Dark cells remain refined polished black silicon. Metallic microstructure, realistic wafer engineering aesthetics, not white chalk or pixel toy. Silver cells should be clearly bright enough to read as 1.
Text verbatim: small discreet "aws" and "UG LEADER" inscriptions in unused dark cells at lower right, never giant.
Avoid: round wafer, floating tiles, holes, mascot, extra decorations, busy text, rainbow, gold numeral, white plastic, solid superimposed number, margins or padding, thick ornamental border, perspective.
