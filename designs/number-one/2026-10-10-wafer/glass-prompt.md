Keep the 1 tall, large, centered and clearly legible. There is NO separate superimposed numeral, plaque, outline, printed number, or floating tile.
Selected bright cells: polished glassy iridescent thin-film silicon, subtle metallic spectral gradients from teal green through pale champagne to violet, crisp glasslike reflections within each individual cell. Controlled premium material, physically plausible silicon colors, not rainbow candy, not neon, absolutely no glow. Unselected cells stay very dark black/charcoal.
Text (verbatim): tiny "aws" and "UG LEADER" inscribed discreetly in unused dark cells at lower-right, secondary to the number, no giant text.
